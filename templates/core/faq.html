Volunteer Fees

Placement fees by duration.

Fees cover accommodation, meals, programme coordination, and operational support. All amounts are in USD.

1 Week

$800

Short immersion — ideal for orientation, site visits, and single-programme contribution.

2 Weeks

$1,200

Two-week engagement allows deeper involvement in daily programme activities and community routines.

4 Weeks (1 Month)

$1,600

One full month — the recommended minimum for meaningful contribution and cultural integration.

8 Weeks (2 Months)

$2,800

Two months allow you to take ownership of programme workstreams and build lasting relationships.

12 Weeks (3 Months)

$4,000

Full-quarter placement — deepest immersion available with leadership and mentorship responsibilities.

Extended Stays

+$300 / extra week

Staying longer than 8 weeks? Each additional week beyond the 8-week placement is $300.

Private room upgrade A private room is available for an additional $50 per week on top of the standard placement fee. Standard accommodation is shared.

Inclusions

What your placement fee covers.

Included

  • Accommodation on-site or nearby (shared room standard)
  • Three meals per day
  • Airport pickup and drop-off (Meru or Nairobi)
  • Orientation and cultural briefing on arrival
  • Dedicated volunteer coordinator throughout your stay
  • Programme materials and tools
  • 24/7 on-ground support from the Tiriji team
  • Certificate of participation on completion

Not Included

  • International flights to and from Kenya
  • Travel and medical insurance (required)
  • Kenya entry visa fees (if applicable)
  • Personal spending money
  • Optional excursions or safaris
  • Specialist equipment for medical or technical placements

How It Works

The application process, step by step.

01 Apply Online

Complete the volunteer application form on our website, choosing your preferred programme and dates.

02 Pay Placement Fee

Review your placement fee and pay via M-Pesa, card, PayPal, or bank transfer. Payment secures your spot.

03 Under Review

Our team checks programme fit, preferred dates, and safety requirements. We usually respond within 5 business days.

04 Approved

You receive a confirmation email with your orientation pack, arrival guide, and what to bring.

05 Arrive & Contribute

Touch down in Meru, meet your coordinator, and begin your placement with full on-ground support.

Common Questions

Detailed answers to frequent questions.

Who can volunteer with Tiriji Foundation?

Anyone aged 18 and over is welcome to apply. Most of our programmes do not require specialist skills — a willingness to contribute, learn, and engage respectfully with the local community is what matters most. Specific programmes (such as health or vocational training support) may have relevant experience requirements, which are noted on each programme page.

What languages do I need to speak?

English is spoken widely in Meru and within our programmes. Swahili is the national language and learning even a few phrases is appreciated by the community. Orientation includes a brief cultural and language primer to help you settle in.

When is the best time to volunteer?

We accept volunteers year-round. The long dry seasons (January–March and July–October) are generally the most comfortable for travel. The rainy seasons (April–June and November–December) are still viable and field programmes continue, but roads can be harder to navigate. Discuss your preferred dates with us during the application process.

How much notice do I need to give before arriving?

We ask for at least four weeks' notice to ensure we can prepare your placement, coordinate logistics, and match you with the right programme activity. For longer placements of two months or more, six to eight weeks' lead time is preferred.

What is the refund policy if I have to cancel?

Refund requests are considered on a case-by-case basis. As a general rule, placement fees are non-refundable once a spot has been confirmed — programme preparation, coordination, and logistics costs are incurred in advance. If you need to reschedule, we will do our best to accommodate a new start date. Please contact us as early as possible if your plans change.

Do I need a visa to enter Kenya?

Most nationalities require a visa to enter Kenya. Kenya operates an e-visa system at evisa.go.ke. A tourist visa is typically sufficient for volunteer stays under 90 days. Check your country's specific requirements and apply well in advance. Visa costs are not included in the placement fee.

Is travel insurance required?

Yes. Tiriji Foundation requires all volunteers to hold valid travel and medical insurance for the full duration of their stay. We recommend comprehensive cover that includes emergency evacuation. Proof of insurance is requested before your placement is confirmed.

What vaccinations do I need?

Consult your GP or a travel health clinic at least six to eight weeks before departure. Commonly recommended vaccinations for Kenya include hepatitis A and B, typhoid, and rabies. Yellow fever vaccination is required if you are travelling from a country with risk of yellow fever transmission. Malaria prophylaxis is strongly advised for Meru County.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept payment via M-Pesa (mobile money — most common for Kenya-based payers), Visa/Mastercard, PayPal, and bank transfer. Your payment summary page will show all available options and step-by-step instructions once your application is submitted. Payment in full is required to confirm your placement.

Can I choose which programme I work on?

Yes. Our application form lets you indicate a programme preference. We do our best to place you where your interests and skills are most useful. During busy periods, we may suggest an alternative that is a strong fit. The final placement is confirmed during the review stage.

Can I extend my placement after arriving?

Yes, extensions are often possible subject to availability. The additional rate for weeks beyond your initial 8-week placement is $300 per week. Extensions shorter than 8 weeks are negotiated directly with your coordinator on arrival.

What is the accommodation like?

Volunteers are housed on-site or in nearby facilities in Kithoka, Meru. Standard accommodation is shared rooms with basic amenities. A private room upgrade is available for $50 per week on request. Meals — three per day — are included and prepared by our kitchen team using local ingredients.
